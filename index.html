This build saves vocal takes on this device, offers WAV conversion, an experimental note-contour chart and a synthetic soulful piano and warm-pad accompaniment. Detected vocal notes are used to add a quiet piano harmony line on the same timeline; pitch tracking is experimental and does not guarantee perfect musical transcription. Original-vocal and backing-mix downloads are separate. This is not AI-generated music or a full production. Saved takes stay in this browser's local storage and are not uploaded.
